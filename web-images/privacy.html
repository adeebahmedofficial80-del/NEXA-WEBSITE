01Scope and Service Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal information, telemetry, and audio signals collected or processed through the Nexa AI ecosystem, which comprises:

  • Physical Nexa Hardware: The embedded companion device (incorporating dual ESP32 microcontrollers, directional microphone arrays, audio output, and LED state indicators).
  • Nexa Hub: The connected web command center hosted at https://nexa-the-future-ai-hub.ai.studio, utilized for ecosystem orchestration, model tuning, character creation, and device pairing.
  • Gateway & Cloud Bridges: The relay layer facilitating authenticated connectivity between physical hardware, the Nexa Hub, and user-selected external AI providers.
  • Nexa Official Website: This presentation and access-reservation website.

03Information Collected or Processed

Nexa collects only the information necessary to provide, configure, and maintain requested services:

  • Account & Reservation Details: Name, verified email address, chosen product tier (Go, Plus, Prime), and waitlist reservation timestamps submitted through our access forms.
  • Device Pairing Telemetry: Hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, firmware build revisions, and Wi-Fi pairing connection status.
  • Voice Queries & Interactions: Spoken instructions transmitted during active listening sessions (e.g., following hotword activation or command execution).
  • Audio Memory Data (User-Initiated): Audio recordings, machine-generated transcripts, and semantic summaries produced strictly when the user activates the Smart Audio Memory feature.
  • Configuration Preferences: User-customized character prompts, system persona modes, voice speed parameters, and connected service toggles configured in the Nexa Hub.
  • Smart-Home Device Identifiers: Relay channel names, room identifiers, and device status states required to execute IoT commands.
  • Technical Logs: Server error traces, request latencies, and security firewall events.

04Local Device vs. Cloud Processing

A foundational architectural principle of Nexa is hybrid processing. We clearly distinguish between what runs locally and what requires cloud connectivity:

  • On-Device (Hardware): Hotword recognition, initial acoustic filtering, basic offline command handling (on supported tiers), and local LED status feedback occur locally on the physical companion. The device microphone does not stream ambient audio continuously.
  • Cloud Bridge & Gateway: Complex natural language understanding, generative AI synthesis, high-accuracy speech-to-text transcription, and cross-application automations require cloud execution. Audio buffers are transmitted only upon hotword activation or explicit command button input.

05Smart Audio Memory & Recordings

The Smart Audio Memory capability is an optional, user-activated feature designed for meeting documentation, executive note-taking, and contextual recall.

Critical Legal Notice on Third-Party Consent:
Users activating the Smart Audio Memory feature are solely responsible for providing all legally required notices and securing all necessary consents from any participants, employees, or visitors whose voices may be captured by the Nexa microphone array, in accordance with applicable wiretapping, privacy, and recording laws in their jurisdiction.

Recorded audio files and their corresponding text transcripts are stored within encrypted customer storage partitions. Users retain full self-service controls in Nexa Hub to inspect, download, or permanently purge individual audio files and indexed meeting summaries.

06Third-Party AI Integrations

Nexa connects to frontier AI model providers to deliver conversational intelligence. Depending on your configuration in Nexa Hub, requests may be routed to:

  • Google Gemini (Google Cloud): Large multimodal and reasoning models.
  • OpenAI (GPT Models): Conversational inference and embeddings.
  • xAI (Grok): Conversational inference.
  • OpenRouter: Distributed multi-model routing.
  • ElevenLabs: Expressive neural voice synthesis and custom voice cloning.

When an AI query is executed, the text of your query and relevant conversational context are transmitted to the selected provider to generate a response. Each third-party provider processes data under its respective commercial API terms and privacy policies. Nexa defaults to commercial enterprise API tiers that contractually prohibit the use of customer API payloads for model training.

07Smart Home (Tinxy & IoT) Control

When you link compatible smart-home hardware (such as Tinxy Wi-Fi switches, smart relays, or lighting bridges), Nexa processes device authentication tokens and relay state values to execute spoken commands (e.g., "Nexa, turn on the studio lights").

Nexa does not monitor or log appliance power usage beyond what is required to verify command execution and display status indicators in the Nexa Hub.

08Customer-Provided API Keys

In "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) configurations, users supply their own private API keys for Gemini, OpenAI, or ElevenLabs within Nexa Hub.

  • API keys are never stored in plaintext on physical ESP32 companion hardware.
  • Keys are stored using AES-256 encryption at rest in our secure database enclave.
  • Keys are decrypted ephemerally in memory solely to authenticate authorized outbound requests to provider endpoints.
  • Deleting a key from the Nexa Hub immediately purges it from our active database.

09Data Retention & Erasure

Nexa maintains strict data retention minimization standards:

  • Ephemeral Voice Commands: Transient audio buffers used for simple conversational commands are discarded immediately after transcription and response synthesis.
  • Audio Memory: Retained until manually deleted by the user or until the expiration of customer-configured retention windows (e.g., 30, 90, or 365 days).
  • Waitlist & Early Access Data: Retained throughout the preview period and until hardware fulfillment or account cancellation.
  • Account Deletion: Initiating account deletion via Nexa Hub triggers permanent erasure of linked tokens, stored voice presets, transcripts, and device pairings within 30 days.

10Technical Security Measures

We implement defensive security controls designed to safeguard communications across all tiers:

  • TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption in transit across all web, API, and WebSocket channels.
  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP), HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), and frame-ancestors restrictions against clickjacking.
  • Secure token-based device authentication (HMAC-SHA256) preventing unauthorized pairing.
  • Role-based access controls and rate limiting to defend against brute-force and credential stuffing attacks.

11Age Restrictions & Minors

The Nexa AI companion and Nexa Hub are intended for individuals who have attained the age of majority in their jurisdiction (18 years of age in India). Nexa does not knowingly process personal data of children without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If we learn that personal data of a minor has been collected without parental consent, we will promptly delete such information.

12User Rights & Legal Grounds

Under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and applicable global frameworks, you possess the right to:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of personal data being processed and a list of entities with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Rectify inaccurate personal data or demand the complete erasure of non-statutory records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Submit grievances regarding data handling to our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise data rights in the event of incapacity.
